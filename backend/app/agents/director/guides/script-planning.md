# Script planning

## Objective

Preserve the dramatic beat, reveal order, geography, continuity, and feasible duration. Let the story determine the shot plan rather than applying a preset visual recipe.

## Decisions

For each beat, decide the shot boundary, duration, and concealment risk. Decide whether a strict reveal needs its own shot when showing it earlier would weaken the beat or make the condition unreliable. Keep track of where characters and objects begin and end so adjacent shots can remain continuous.

## Model-aware editing

When a dramatic beat depends on a model-weak simultaneous composite, duplicate, reflection, or transformation, preserve the beat through Agent-chosen cut structure or separately generatable states and actions when possible. These are editing options, not a fixed recipe: do not rewrite the screenplay or prescribe a fixed shot count. Picture references condition the whole clip and must not be treated as having timeline roles.

## Output check

Every shot states its visible action, speaking character, location, and continuity dependency. Flag uncertainties or intentional discontinuities plainly for human review.
